Our cattle

Aberdeen Angus & Black Limousin

The herd

The heart of the farm

Today we have a mixed herd of Aberdeen Angus and Black Limousin cattle.

They graze the fields around the farm, and looking after them is our year-round job, long after the last sweetcorn has been picked. During the summer we run farm tours, so you can come and meet them.

We farm regeneratively, and the cattle are a big part of that, grazing the grass and feeding the soil that our crops grow in.

Aberdeen Angus

A native breed from the north-east of Scotland. Angus cattle are naturally hornless, hardy and calm, do well outdoors on grass, and are known for well-marbled, full-flavoured beef.

Black Limousin

Limousin cattle come from the Limousin region of central France, where they're traditionally golden-red. Black Limousins are the black-coated line of the breed: muscular, strong, and known for lean beef.

Out in the fields

Grazing at Llannant

Through the year

Silage, hay & haylage

Summer isn't just sweetcorn. We make our own silage, hay and haylage to feed the herd through the winter.

When the weather's right we cut the grass, turn it and bale it. How long it dries in the field decides what it becomes:

  • Silage: wilted briefly, baled while the grass is still moist, then wrapped so it keeps.
  • Haylage: left to dry a bit longer, then baled and wrapped.
  • Hay: dried right out in the sun, and baled dry.

It all means good feed for the cattle through the winter, grown on our own fields.
