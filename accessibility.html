Accessibility statement

We want everyone to be able to use this website and enjoy a visit to the farm. This page explains how accessible the website is, what we're still working on, and how to get help.

Last updated: 9 November 2026

Using this website

We aim to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 at level AA. You should be able to:

  • zoom in up to 400% with the text still readable and nothing cut off
  • use the whole website with just a keyboard, including the menu and the photo viewer
  • skip straight to the main content with the "Skip to main content" link
  • use a screen reader: every photo has a short description, and the season calendar is also given as text
  • read text with enough colour contrast
  • turn off animation: if your device is set to reduce motion, the website does too
  • use the website on a phone or tablet in either orientation

What isn't fully accessible yet

  • Old newspaper cuttings and the 1941 sale poster on the History page are photos of printed text. We've described each one. If you'd like the full wording, email us and we'll send it.
  • The old film on the History page has no captions or audio description. It plays from YouTube, whose player we don't control.
  • Booking pages are run by Ticket Tailor and aren't part of this website. If you have trouble booking, email us and we'll book for you.

Visiting the farm

Llannant is a working farm, so some areas aren't level. To help you plan:

  • You park on the big concrete yard as you come into the farm.
  • The pick-your-own patch is a short walk down from the yard, on a grass and earth track. The field itself is uneven and can be muddy, so wellies or sturdy boots are best.
  • Toilets: please confirm whether there are any, and whether they're accessible.
  • Assistance dogs are welcome. Please keep them close, as there are cattle and other dogs on the farm.

If you have mobility needs or anything else we can help with, email us before you come and we'll do what we can, for example by helping you get closer to the patch.

Asking for information in another format

If you need anything on this website in a different format, such as large print or plain text, email contact@llannantfarm.com or call 07973 712852. We'll reply within 5 working days.

Reporting a problem

We're always looking to improve. If you find something on this website that's hard to use, or think we're not meeting these standards, please email contact@llannantfarm.com and tell us which page and what went wrong.

How we checked this website

This statement was prepared on 9 November 2026. We checked the website by using it with only a keyboard, at high zoom and on phone-sized screens. We also ran automated checks for colour contrast, missing image descriptions and page structure, and fixed the problems they found.
