Your information

Privacy policy

We're a family farm, not a data company. This page explains, in plain English, what personal information we collect, why, and what your rights are.

Last updated: 9 November 2026

The short version

  • This website doesn't use cookies, adverts or tracking.
  • We only get personal information when you email, phone or text us, or book a slot or farm tour through Ticket Tailor.
  • We only use it to run your visit and reply to you, and we never sell it.
  • We'll only email you about opening days if you ask us to, and you can stop that at any time.

Who we are

This website is run by Llannant Farm, the Stevens family farm at Llannant Farm, Penyrheol, Gorseinon, Swansea SA4 4ND. We're the "data controller" for the personal information described here.

Contact us about anything on this page by email at contact@llannantfarm.com, by phone on 07973 712852, or by post at the address above.

What we collect and why

When you visit this website

We don't ask you for anything and we don't set cookies. Our website host, Cloudflare, handles technical information such as your IP address and browser type so it can deliver the pages and protect the site from attacks. If we switch on Cloudflare's visitor statistics, these count visits without cookies and without identifying you.

Everything on our pages, including the fonts and photos, comes from our own website, so your browser doesn't contact Google or any other company while you look round.

When you email, phone or text us

We use your name, your email address or phone number, and whatever you tell us to reply to you. Our reason (the "lawful basis") is our legitimate interest in answering questions about the farm.

When you book a pick-your-own slot or a farm tour

Bookings are made through Ticket Tailor, which processes them on our behalf. When you book, we receive your name, your email address, the number of people in your car and the time slot you chose. We use these to manage your visit, contact you about your booking (for example if we have to close for bad weather) and check you in on the day. Our lawful basis is that we need the information to provide the booking you asked for.

Ticket Tailor's own privacy policy, on tickettailor.com, explains how they look after booking information.

If you ask to hear when we open

When you book, you can tick a box to get an email when pick your own opens each season. We'll only send these if you've ticked the box (our lawful basis is your consent). Every email has a link to stop them, or you can just email us.

Reviews

Our Reviews page shows reviews that visitors have posted publicly on our Facebook page, with their first name and the initial of their last name only. If you'd like your review taken off our website, email us and we'll remove it.

Other websites and services

  • YouTube: the old film on our History page only loads from YouTube when you press play. After that, YouTube (owned by Google) may set cookies and collect information under its own privacy policy.
  • Links to Facebook, Instagram, Google Maps and Ticket Tailor take you to their websites, where their own privacy policies apply.

Who we share it with

We don't sell or share your information for marketing. It's only handled by the services that help us run the website and bookings: Cloudflare (hosting), Ticket Tailor (bookings and opening emails) and our email provider. Some of these may store information outside the UK, with the safeguards UK law requires.

How long we keep it

  • Emails: up to two years, unless we need them longer to deal with a problem.
  • Booking details: until the end of the following season, then deleted.
  • The "tell me when you open" list: until you unsubscribe.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law you can ask us to:

  • give you a copy of the information we hold about you
  • correct anything that's wrong
  • delete your information
  • stop or limit how we use it, or object to us using it
  • give your information to you, or another organisation, in a usable format

If you gave us consent (for example for opening emails), you can withdraw it at any time. To use any of these rights, email contact@llannantfarm.com. We'll reply within one month.

Complaints

If you're unhappy with how we've used your information, please tell us first and we'll try to put it right. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) at ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint or on 0303 123 1113.

Changes to this policy

If we change how we use personal information, we'll update this page and the date at the top.
